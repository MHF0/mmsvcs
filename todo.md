4. **src/pages/Contact.tsx** - Contact form and company information

7. **src/components/Footer.tsx** - Footer with contact info and links
8. **src/App.tsx** - Update routing for all pages

- Responsive design for mobile/desktop
- Professional branding with MM SVCS logo
- Service-focused content
- Contact forms for lead generation
- Texas location emphasis
